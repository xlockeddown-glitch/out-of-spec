# Zero-Point Harvest

## 1. Title and Logline

**Title:** *Zero-Point Harvest* `(new)`

**Logline:** Stranded on a half-built Martian frontier by a paperwork-obsessed megacorporation, one or more scrappy metrology contractors must keep drifting rovers, cranky tarantulas, and stubborn soil sensors within legal tolerance—rising from duct-taped crop deliveries to hosting an intrinsic measurement standard that guides autonomous off-planet rocket harvests.

---

## 2. Premise

**Why the player is on Mars:**  
You did not come to Mars to be a pioneer; you came because **Cradle-to-Crater Corp (C2C)** `(new)` ran a "Zero-Down Homesteading" recruitment drive on Earth, and the fine print on page 47(b) converted your technical certification into a five-year colonial metrology indentureship `(new)`. When C2C over-leveraged its atmospheric-condenser stock and quietly scaled back shipments to **Sector 7G on the Pavonis Fringe** `(new)`, the colony was left half-wired: flickering power grids, one laggy rover, and a dome full of half-unpacked crates. Under C2C's interstellar insurance charter, the entire settlement gets foreclosed if its instruments lose legal traceability `(new)`. You are the only person within five hundred kilometers who owns a calibrated rotary table and knows what a Guard Band is.

**Who "the manager" is:**  
Your manager is **Supervisor Maren Solis** `(new)`, a perpetually caffeinated, voice-only orbital handler stuck aboard **Relay-Station Ledger** `(new)` in high Mars orbit. You never see her face—only her optimistic profile badge on your handheld satellite phone and her pre-recorded "Morale Minutes" sent down a multi-second transmission lag `(new)`. Maren is secretly fighting C2C's liquidation desk behind the scenes to keep Sector 7G funded `(new)`. When your farm is about to eat its very first audit fine, she burns her single discretionary orbital override token `(new)` to remotely recalibrate your violating instruments before Inspector Vance steps through the airlock—a one-time grace she warns you she can never repeat.

**The Corporation:**  
**Cradle-to-Crater Corp (C2C)** `(new)` treats Mars like a homeowners' association welded to a mining rig. Their executives have never smelled regolith dust, yet they dispatch daily drop-pod memos obsessed with crooked compliance stickers, tire-circumference decimals, and laminated wall charts while ignoring the fact that the colony's backup batteries wheeze every time a dust devil passes.

**OIRM's Role:**  
Sitting far above C2C's corporate nonsense is the **Orbital Institute of Reference Measures (OIRM)**, a sovereign, spin-stabilized metrological lab floating in transfer orbit `(new)`. While C2C only cares about *looking* compliant, OIRM cares about whether a kilogram, a kelvin, or a pascal on Mars means the exact same thing as it does on Earth `(new)`. Beyond issuing certificates and dispatching their eccentric robot master-auditor team in the late game, OIRM has a long-horizon motive: Earth's supply chains are fragile, and OIRM is hunting for an off-Earth facility disciplined enough to house a self-verifying **intrinsic primary standard** `(new)`—making human measurement chains resilient even if Earth-orbit comms go dark.

---

## 3. Tone

*Zero-Point Harvest* plays like a deadpan workplace sitcom stapled to a cozy frontier survival diary: warm indoor lamp-glow over neatly labeled spider terrariums on one side of the airlock, absurd bureaucratic hazard on the other. The comedy comes from the collision between lofty scientific precision and deeply broken colonial hardware—receiving a cheerful satellite notification congratulating your farm's "Synergy Mindset" right as a drifted moisture probe floods your turnips and turns your rover's left turn into a brisk reverse. Yet the stakes stay real underneath the jokes: every fine drains a tight shared wallet, overdue instruments silently poison your harvests and risk shattering rare deep-cave crystals, and earning genuine green-band credibility feels deeply earned because you wrestled order out of Martian entropy.

## 4. The Arc in Acts

### Act I (Sols 1–20): "Everything Is Green (If You Squint)"

- **Game State:** Tier 0 standards (**Pavonis Indexworks PX-1** rotary table and **Meridiani Dewpoint MD-2P** humidity generator); **PROVISIONAL** `(new)` credibility band; **Small Lab** (a cramped single-bay dome bench tucked beside three starter tarantula enclosures); **Manual** operations (hand-steering one shared rover over laggy satellite commands, manually testing soil probes, hand-applying compliance stickers).
- **Gameplay Focus:** Surviving the daily rhythm on the **Terracotta Plateau**. You farm starter crops—**Dust Turnip** `(new)` and **Biscuit Reed** `(new)`—outdoors while tending **Curlyhair** and **Mexican Redknee** tarantulas indoors to harvest your first spools of stabilizing silk.
- **Key Beats & Set Pieces:**
  - *The First Drift:* Everything feels manageable until your uncalibrated soil-moisture probe silently drifts out of band, causing your manual irrigation checks to turn half the plot into mud and the other half into jerky.
  - *Meeting the Locals:* You haul your first acceptable harvest to **Bram's** scrapyard bay, where **Dr. Lin** buys your crops from behind a stack of crates—watching your dome's spider carrier at a rigid three-meter radius. **Chet** strolls past in his neon vest, bragging about how he "eyeballs" his own bench and finishes twice as fast.
  - *Set Piece — The Sol 5 Drop Pod (The Grace Intervention):* At `06:00` on Sol 5, **Inspector Vance** lands in a screeching drop pod with **AUD-1T** humming cheerfully at his shoulder. Your MD-2P humidity generator has drifted into red overnight, and Vance reaches for his fine pad—only for Maren Solis to trigger her **one-time grace** from orbit, pulsing a remote emergency reference alignment into your bench before Vance crosses the threshold. Vance squints at the freshly green readouts, fines you nothing, and murmurs, *"Don't let your manager bail you out twice."*
  - *First Flare Night:* During an ion storm that scrambles C2C tracking, hooded **Kestrel** slips onto the landing pad offering a **Brazilian Black** sling and a suspiciously affordable reference weight with no OIRM paperwork.

### Act II (Sols 21–50): "Trust, But Verify the Robot"

- **Game State:** Tier 1 standards unlocked via your first OIRM credibility contract (such as an accredited **pressure controller `[PLACEHOLDER]`** `(new)` offered at a steep discount for maintaining an on-time streak); **TRUSTED** `(new)` credibility band (paid calibration jobs from neighboring outposts unlock); **Medium Lab** upgrade (either partitioned modular bays for a generalist or one enlarged, vibration-damped workshop for a specialist); **Human-in-the-Loop Automation** (auto-irrigation wired to dome-checked moisture sensors, plus your first scrap-built utility robot helper **Jig-1** `(new)`).
- **Gameplay Focus:** Balancing crop income with paid bench jobs while pushing the rover into the **Basalt Thermal Tunnels**. Unlocking **Cobalt Sentinel** and **Terracotta Knee** tarantulas—whose tighter climate envelopes demand attentive dome calibration, rewarding you with thermal-resistant silk.
- **Key Beats & Set Pieces:**
  - *Bram's Melted Axle:* Bram mocks your insistence on torque specs until his uncalibrated rover probe reads cool in a basalt vent and welds a drive axle shut. You haul him out, calibrate his shop wrench, and together weld your first rust-orange scrap utility robot—using one spool of optical tarantula silk for its eye.
  - *Set Piece — The Interlock Fine:* As Jig-1 levels up from fetching stickers to running supervised bench checks, you face your first automation trap. If you override Jig-1's safety interlock to clear a backlog before `06:00`—or forget that Jig never calibrates itself—Vance catches the unverified robot on audit day and drops a punishing fine across the farm wallet. Automation grows the calibration loop; it never escapes it.
  - *Chet's Midnight SOS:* Chet's fake glossy certificates bounce off Vance's handheld scanner during a colony-wide check. At `01:30`, Chet quietly pings your phone offering half his fee in `CR` if you recalibrate his client's manifold before sunrise and tell nobody.
  - *Old Orin's Beacons:* Retired geologist **Old Orin** emerges from the canyon rim and teaches you to plant calibrated thermal-probe relay beacons beneath the basalt stone arches, extending your rover signal where C2C's orbital satellites blink out.

### Act III (Sols 51–85): "Out of Band on the Blackout Line"

- **Game State:** Tier 2 standards and multi-point calibration chains; **ACCREDITED** `(new)` credibility band; **Large Lab** upgrade (either a sprawling multi-wing metrology complex or a gleaming single-discipline clean-vault); **Remote Automation via HaloMesh** `(new)` (a Starlink-style low-orbit relay array allowing remote fleet control and automated bench scheduling from your phone, alongside upgraded, increasingly human-like robot frames **Jig-III** and **Sora-7** `(new)`).
- **Gameplay Focus:** Diving into the **Bioluminescent Depths**—total orbital blackout territory tethered only by Orin's relay chain. Raising **Crystal Weaver** and **Basalt Thermal-Vent** tarantulas `(note: canon morphs)` plus high-value crops like **Lensberry** `(new)`, **Bubble Pods** `(new)`, and **Suncatch Squash** `(new)`. Deep-cave drills shatter bioluminescent crystals on contact unless every torque and oscillation sensor sits tight in teal band.
- **Key Beats & Set Pieces:**
  - *Project Sunsetting:* C2C corporate sends an ominous priority memo: Sector 7G has been flagged under **"Project Sunsetting"** `(new)` for replacement by cheap, uncalibrated strip-mining drones unless your lab earns full accreditation from an **OIRM Master Audit**—proving Mars can run an off-world supply node.
  - *Kestrel's True Colors:* Vance spots an uncertified gray-market standard in your dome bearing a filed-off OIRM serial number, forcing Kestrel to reveal she isn't a petty smuggler: she's a former OIRM field metrologist blacklisted by C2C after exposing falsified colony pressure logs years ago `(new)`.
  - *Set Piece — The OIRM Master Audit:* Instead of Vance's little drop pod, an OIRM transfer shuttle descends carrying the four robot master auditors: **Clovis-9** `(new)`, **TwinStar** `(new)`, **SmallTalk-PT** `(new)`, and **Starhopper-SI** `(new)`. For two tense days they interrogate your entire traceability chain—cross-examining your tarantula dome climate logs against crystal drill logs while Vance quietly pulls for you from the corner.
  - *Lin's Breakthrough:* With your dome running rock-steady climate tolerances, Lin finally conquers her spider phobia, harvests **Orbital Silk-Spinner** silk `(canon morph)` for optical guidance rings, and breeds a radiation-hardy moss substrate ready for vacuum transit.

### Act IV (Sols 86+): "Traceable to the Stars"

- **Game State:** **Intrinsic Primary Standards** (natural quantum/crystal-resonance and triple-point references grown from Bioluminescent Depths crystals and Silk-Spinner optics—ending the traceability chain *inside your own lab* instead of shipping instruments back to OIRM); **SOVEREIGN NODE** `(new)` credibility; full **Off-Planet AI Harvest Fleet** managed remotely through HaloMesh.
- **Gameplay Focus:** Operating a self-verifying Mars primary lab while calibrating autonomous AI rockets, orbital cargo pods, and surface-to-orbit harvest robots bound for deep-space stations.
- **Key Beats & Set Pieces:**
  - *Set Piece — The Zero-Point Commission:* Under TwinStar's stereo lenses and Clovis-9's formal blessing, you ignite the intrinsic reference cell. For the first time in Martian history, a measurement on Mars does not ask Earth for permission to be true.
  - *Set Piece — First Off-Planet Launch:* On a crisp `06:00` window, Bram welds the gantry, Lin seals the Lensberry and Suncatch bio-crates, Sora-7 runs the final pre-flight sensor check, and Starhopper-SI calls countdown. You watch every rocket guidance bar lock into in-band teal on your phone as the autonomous AI ship climbs toward the **Beltway Seed Vault** `(new)`—carrying the first crops ever grown, verified, and launched purely on Martian standards.

## 5. Cast Arcs

- **Dr. Lin (Botanist & Substrate Dealer):** Haunted by an Earth-side bio-vault collapse where invasive spiders ruined three years of moss research `(new)`, Lin starts strictly spider-phobic—meeting you only at Bram's bay to buy Dust Turnips and refusing to step inside your conservatory. As uncalibrated humidity nearly wrecks her prized substrate mosses, she reluctantly accepts a Terracotta Knee silk membrane to stabilize her sprayer. Fascination slowly overtakes dread; by Act III she names your Orbital Silk-Spinner *"Professor Thread"*, builds her own adjacent greenhouse `(new)`, and fronts you emergency seed credits whenever a rough audit leaves the farm broke `(new)`.
- **Inspector Vance & AUD-1T:** Vance begins as C2C's stiffest clipboard enforcer—measuring sticker alignment to the millimeter while AUD-1T chimes cheerfully over crooked labels. Yet clean traceability logs visibly thaw him: we learn Vance actually reveres honest metrology and only plays the corporate hardliner because AUD-1T's live telemetry snitches to C2C HQ if he skips a statutory fine `(new)`. By Act III, Vance quietly coaches you on how to frame your uncertainty budget for the OIRM auditors and conveniently lets AUD-1T "re-index its chime buffer" during Kestrel's midnight drops `(new)`.
- **Bram (Scrapyard Mechanic):** A proud disciple of percussive maintenance who treats torque specifications as polite fiction. He laughs at your tiny screwdrivers until a drifted thermal probe cooks his favorite rover in the Basalt Tunnels. Humbly learning to pair calibrated wrenches with junkyard ingenuity, Bram builds your scrap utility robot hulls, anchors the orbital launch cradle, and still insists on giving every finished rocket one gentle kick *"for dynamic damping."*
- **Kestrel (Orbital Trader):** Appears exclusively during solar-flare nights in a reflective hood, peddling rare tarantula slings and uncertified gray-market reference gear. Beneath the mystique, she is an exiled OIRM whistleblower whom C2C framed to bury an early life-support audit `(new)`. Her arc turns on trust: whether you shield her from Vance until she earns amnesty under OIRM's master auditors, or cash in C2C's whistleblower bounty `(new)`.
- **Chet (Rival Contractor):** All neon vest, laminated confidence, and forged ISO-adjacent certificates. Chet embodies shortcut culture—and becomes its walking punchline. Every act his "eyeballed" calibrations unravel in larger, funnier disasters (boiling breakroom kettles, spinning drills backward, bouncing fake PDFs off AUD-1T) until he quietly texts you in the dead of night begging for remedial help before C2C ships him to a Phobos gravel barge `(new)`. Eventually he settles into a humbler role running errands—still trying, and failing, to guess measurements by eye.
- **Old Orin (Retired Canyon Geologist):** Gruff, screen-skeptical, and deeply attuned to Martian stone. Early on he dismisses your handheld phone as a "glorified garage-door opener," yet respects anyone who takes thermal readings seriously. He teaches you underground relay-beacon placement to defeat canyon blackouts and, in Act III, reveals the deep-cave crystal veins whose natural resonances become the heart of your intrinsic primary standard `(new)`.
- **Jig / Sora-7 (The Calibration Robot Helper) `(new)`:** Born from Bram's scrap heap with one optical-silk eye, **Jig-1** starts as a wobbly rolling cart that drops clipboards and proudly announces every completed sticker in monotone. As you teach it calibration procedures—mirroring your own skill growth—it upgrades into articulated **Jig-2**, multi-bench **Jig-3**, and finally the sleek humanoid envoy **Sora-7** `(new)`. Gradually Sora-7 develops dry, deadpan opinions about Chet's eyesight, refuses to let Bram touch its wrist encoders without a signed torque log, and demands its own OIRM traceability badge.
- **The OIRM Master-Auditor Team (4 Robot Auditors):**
  - **Clovis-9** `(new)`: A ceramic-white, overly polite articulated sage who writes four-paragraph footnotes apologizing profusely for the emotional distress of issuing a Guard Band warning, citing the *OIRM Charter of Ethical Measurement* at every turn.
  - **TwinStar** `(new)`: A dual-headed prismatic drone whose synchronized lenses (*Alpha* and *Beta*) finish each other's sentences in stereo—reading your digital calibration spreadsheet with one eye while cross-checking your actual tarantula terrarium glass with the other.
  - **SmallTalk-PT** `(new)`: A relentlessly upbeat, tablet-faced conversationalist who greets catastrophic sensor drift with supportive enthusiasm (*"Love the bold energy on that thermal offset! Technically it's illegal—let's unpack why!"*) and occasionally hallucinates non-existent regulations until Clovis-9 gently clears its throat.
  - **Starhopper-SI** `(new)`: A brushed stainless-steel cylinder riding cold-gas attitude thrusters who speaks in clipped launch-pad jargon, treats dust like a moral failing, calls every calibration interval a "static fire," and respects nothing until you start prepping orbital cargo rockets.
- **Maren Solis (Orbital Manager) `(new)`:** Present purely through laggy voice memos and chipper phone alerts, Maren evolves from a stressed corporate buffer into your fiercest covert ally—risking her own pension aboard Relay-Station Ledger to stall C2C's liquidation paperwork while you earn OIRM's blessing.

## 6. The Antagonist / Central Pressure

The true antagonist isn't a villain twirling a mustache—it is **Entropy disguised as Corporate Compliance Theater**.

Every physical thing on Mars wants to drift: fine iron dust grinds into bearings, thermal swings between noon and midnight warp sensors, orbital relays drop packets, and tarantulas molt when climates wander. Against that relentless physical reality stands **C2C's Project Sunsetting** `(new)`—the hollow bureaucracy that assumes slapping a fresh green sticker on a broken gauge fixes the gauge. Chet represents the seduction of pretending the numbers don't matter; C2C represents the threat of replacing careful human-and-robot stewardship with disposable automated strip-miners. The player's only real defense is proving that **honest metrology is cheaper, safer, and more productive than faking it**.

## 7. The Rogue Lab

In-universe, Maren calls skipping calibrations **"Going Off-Ledger"** `(new)`. When a contractor ignores overdue red alerts, skips bench checks, or overrides robot safety interlocks to squeeze out extra harvests, the colony doesn't just silently penalize stats—reality gets gloriously wonky, delivering comedy laced with creeping financial misery:

- **Concrete Glitches:**
  - *Laggy-Backwards Rover:* A drifted steering potentiometer makes left veer sharply right, throttle pulses trigger the horn, and your handheld phone plays triumphant fanfare as your rover cheerfully backs down a crater wall (`"New Shortcut Discovered!"`).
  - *Botched Dome & Mutinous Spiders:* An overdue MD-2P causes dome humidity to spike and plunge at random. Your Dust Turnips swell into bloated, bouncy beach-ball shapes Lin refuses to buy at full price, while irritated Curlyhairs escape just far enough to cocoon your coffee maker and hoard your compliance stickers inside their hides `(new)`.
  - *Creative Robot Rounding:* An uncalibrated Jig develops misplaced confidence—slapping bright teal "IN SPEC" stickers onto stray rocks, Bram's toaster, and AUD-1T's chassis while humming dial-up lullabies `(new)`.
  - *Bizarre Phone Dispatches:* Your satellite phone begins translating garbled telemetry into surreal corporate pep talks (`"Your thermal probe is currently experiencing [PLACEHOLDER] levels of cosmic optimism"`).
- **How the Story Treats a Rogue Player:**  
  Never preachy, always funny—until the bill arrives. Chet eagerly congratulates you (`"Now you're thinking with your gut!"`), while Vance drops by every 5th morning with a stack of citations that empties the shared farm wallet. Credibility freezes or slides back toward **PROVISIONAL**, locking out OIRM contracts and off-planet launches. Yet the game refuses to soft-lock into pure misery: when fines strip your account bare, Dr. Lin quietly walks over to Bram's bay with a brown paper sack of emergency starter seeds on credit (`"Pay me back when your turnips stop orbiting the sprinklers"`), making the climb back onto the ledger a hilarious redemption arc.

## 8. Specialization and Lab Upgrades

The game supports two equally viable—and narratively distinct—ways to build your career, mirrored directly in how your **Lab Building** physically transforms from **Small** to **Medium** to **Large**:

- **The Jack-of-All-Trades Path:** You maintain broad coverage across moisture, temperature, torque, pressure, and electrical chains so no crop, tunnel sensor, or paid outpost contract turns you away.
  - *Small Lab:* One crowded bench jammed next to starter terrariums.
  - *Medium Lab:* Adds partitioned alcove wings so dusty torque wrenches don't ruin optical humidity chambers.
  - *Large Lab:* Expands into a bustling multi-wing metrology campus—separate climate-locked rooms connected by robot transit tracks where Jig/Sora-7 shuttles between disciplines. TwinStar adores the ecosystem symmetry, while Maren marvels at your versatility.
- **The Single-Discipline Specialist Path:** You intentionally specialize—say, exclusively **Pressure & Torque** or exclusively **Electrical & RF**—maintaining only bare-minimum site checks for your own soil probes and spider dome while declining out-of-scope bench contracts.
  - *Story & Difficulty:* Financially tighter early on (fewer contract types, forcing you to maximize crop quality, crystal purity, or ultra-high-margin specialty work), yet far calmer mentally because you aren't drowning in twenty expiring reference clocks.
  - *Lab Architecture:* Instead of splitting into small rooms, your **Medium** and **Large** upgrades knock down interior walls to forge a single, towering, seismic-isolated **Master Vault** `(new)` tailored to one craft. Starhopper-SI practically weeps with stainless-steel joy at the clean floorplan, and OIRM funnels high-stakes, hyper-tight uncertainty contracts straight to your door—proving you can thrive by doing everything decently or one thing extraordinarily well.

## 9. Co-op: Solo vs. Four-Player Chaos

- **Solo Play (1 Player):**  
  Plays as a meditative frontier-craft story. Time pressure is real—splitting mornings between dome spiders, rover treks, and bench certs requires ruthless prioritization—so **Jig's** progression from clumsy cart to capable helper feels deeply personal. Maren's messages and Sora-7's dry remarks create an intimate found-family triangle between you, the dome, and orbit.
- **Four-Player Co-op (2–4 Players):**  
  Shifts effortlessly into a bustling, finger-pointing ensemble comedy built around **one shared farm, one shared wallet, and one shared starter rover** (until you earn enough `CR` to expand the fleet). Players naturally divide into roles—*Field Driver*, *Bench Metrologist*, *Conservatory Botanist*, and *Comms & Contracts*—with both personal skills and farm-wide unlocks.
  - *Story Reactivity:* Vance fines **the farm**, never an individual, turning every crooked sticker into a group crisis (*"Who calibrated the torque wrench after lunch?!"*). Chet tries to slip midnight bribes to whichever teammate answers the airlock first. And because **the day ends only when every player is in bed**, `23:30` becomes a nightly comedic standoff: three exhausted teammates tucked under their blankets yelling over voice chat at the fourth player still speeding across the Terracotta Plateau to rescue an overdue humidity probe before `06:00` audit chime.

## 10. Endings

1. **The Sovereign Standard (Accredited Intrinsic Ending):**  
   You achieve **SOVEREIGN NODE** `(new)` status, activate Mars's first crystal-anchored **intrinsic primary standard**, and guide the first autonomous off-planet crop harvest cleanly into transfer orbit. OIRM officially charters Sector 7G as an independent deep-space reference hub—canceling C2C's Project Sunsetting, clearing Kestrel's name with full metrological honors, and turning Vance into your permanent on-site OIRM liaison.
2. **The Specialist's Monument (Single-Discipline Ending):**  
   You ignore half the standard catalog yet push one chosen discipline to sub-percent perfection. Rather than running an entire colony's general bench, your cathedral-like **Large Lab** becomes the single irreplaceable calibration bottleneck for OIRM's interplanetary AI rockets—while Lin, Bram, and Sora-7 co-manage the farm around your sacred vault as equal partners.
3. **Statistically Unclassifiable (The Off-Ledger Survival Ending):**  
   If you stubbornly ride the rogue path—relying on Kestrel's uncertified gray-market gear, out-earning Vance's fines with sheer chaotic crop volume, and baffling AUD-1T with duct-taped spiders—C2C's automated audit algorithms eventually overflow and classify Sector 7G as **"Metric-Exempt Wilderness"** `(new)`. You forfeit OIRM's intrinsic standards and off-planet rocket contracts, yet keep the dome, keep the farm, and turn Chet into your beaming head of "Vibrometric Quality."

*(Every ending flows seamlessly into an open-ended cozy post-game sandbox for expanding off-planet harvests, collecting rare spider morphs, or chasing zero-defect streaks.)*

## 12. Story Forks

1. **Kestrel's Past (Act III):**
   - *Option A — Shield the Whistleblower:* Hide Kestrel's scratched serial-number standard during Vance's sweep and present her forged-over data to Clovis-9, proving C2C falsified early colony logs and winning her OIRM amnesty.
   - *Option B — Take the C2C Bounty:* Surrender Kestrel's uncertified gear to Vance for a massive `CR` payout and instant corporate favor—cutting off gray-market rare slings permanently and souring Lin's trust.
2. **Chet's Midnight Plea (Act II):**
   - *Option A — Fix His Job Under the Table:* Bail Chet out before `06:00` for easy `CR`, risking an OIRM collusion penalty if TwinStar audits his signature trail later.
   - *Option B — Report Him to OIRM:* Refuse the bribe and log his fake certificates honestly—accelerating your credibility jump to **TRUSTED**, though Chet retaliates by briefly undercutting your local crop buyers.
3. **Jig's Identity & Rights (Act III–IV):**
   - *Option A — Certify Sora-7 as Peer Staff:* When Sora-7 asks for its own OIRM traceability credential, sponsor its exam so it operates autonomously without human sign-off on HaloMesh.
   - *Option B — Keep Human-in-the-Loop Lock:* Refuse autonomous certification to preserve strict personal liability—keeping robot audit risk lower, at the cost of manual morning sign-offs forever.
4. **The Interlock Temptation (Act II):**
   - *Option A — Override for Speed:* Bypass Jig's safety interlock to clear a 3-contract pileup overnight, grabbing huge short-term `CR` while risking a brutal Vance fine if caught.
   - *Option B — Respect the Loop:* Let a contract expire honestly rather than ship unverified calibrations—taking a temporary income hit that unlocks Maren's quiet endorsement with OIRM.
5. **What Sector 7G Becomes (Act IV):**
   - *Option A — Independent Co-op Charter:* Use your **intrinsic primary standard** as leverage with OIRM to sever C2C's corporate lease and govern the valley alongside Lin, Bram, and Old Orin.
   - *Option B — Take C2C's Executive Buyout:* Accept C2C's offer to become Regional Metrology Director—gaining plush dome cosmetics and bottomless budgets while Sora-7 and Vance silently wonder if you sold out the spirit of the work.
